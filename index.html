First download & privacy

Translation runs in your browser on this device. First use downloads about 150 MB for each English/Japanese direction, about 300 MB for the pair. Other language pairs use a multilingual pack of about 900 MB. Keep Wi-Fi connected and allow time. Older phones may take longer to translate. No paid API key or subscription is required.

Auto, on-device Japanese, and the Japanese fallback download an additional Whisper speech pack on first use and recognize audio on this device. Short phrases can be misidentified; use a manual speaker if needed. Whisper base uses the Apache 2.0 license. Manual microphone recognition and voice playback depend on your browser and installed languages. Your browser’s speech provider may process microphone audio online. If speech is unavailable, type your phrase instead. Keep the app open during a conversation.

The app shell is saved for offline opening. Translation also needs the language models to finish downloading and remain in browser storage. Clearing site data removes downloaded models. Translations may make mistakes.

English ↔ Japanese uses FuguMT: English → Japanese and Japanese → English, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Kadonox provides the ONNX exports of staka’s FuguMT models, trained on JParaCrawl. Other pairs use NLLB-200, licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0. Browser inference uses Transformers.js.
